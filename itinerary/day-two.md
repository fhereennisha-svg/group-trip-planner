Day 2: Science, Nature, and Scenic Views in Göttingen
Dedicate your second day to Göttingen’s rich scientific heritage, local museums, and relaxing green spaces.

Day 2 Itinerary Outline

## Morning (9:30 AM): Visit the Forum Wissen (University of Göttingen's knowledge museum), located in the former zoological institute. It features fascinating interactive exhibits exploring how science shapes our world.

## Late Morning (11:30 AM): Walk through the Old Botanical Garden (Alter Botanischer Garten) right next to the historic campus walls. Stroll past historic greenhouses and tranquil ponds.

## Lunch (1:00 PM): Grab lunch at a nearby spot around the Theaterplatz, such as Café Central or a local bakery.

## Afternoon (2:30 PM): Walk up to the Bismarckturm (Bismarck Tower) on the Hainberg forest ridge overlooking the city for a panoramic view of Göttingen and the Leine Valley.

## Evening (6:30 PM): Enjoy dinner and unwind at a cozy local restaurant in the Nordstadt or inner city, such as Nudelhaus for fresh pasta or a traditional German meal.
