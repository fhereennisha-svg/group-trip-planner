# Our Group Trip
Day 1 Itinerary Outline

## Morning (9:00 AM): Start at the Altes Rathaus (Old Town Hall) in the Marktplatz to see the famous Gänseliesel-Brunnen (Gose Girl Fountain)—traditionally kissed by newly minted PhD students.

## Late Morning (10:30 AM): Walk down the historic Weender Straße pedestrian zone, grab coffee at a local café, and check out the historic St. Johannis Church towers.

## Lunch (12:30 PM): Grab a traditional regional meal at a historic spot like the Bartholomäus or a cozy local bakery downtown.

## Afternoon (2:00 PM): Visit the historic Aula der Universität and explore the surrounding historic campus footprint of the Georg-August-Universität. Walk along the scenic Wallanlagen (the green park path built along the old medieval city wall).

## Evening (6:00 PM): Dinner at the atmospheric Gaudi or a traditional tavern like Paulaner im Bullwert, followed by a relaxed evening stroll through the illuminated old town.
